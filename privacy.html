Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This page explains how SpeedTools handles data — simply, clearly, and honestly.

Overview

SpeedTools is designed to respect user privacy by default. We do not require accounts, we do not run ads, and we do not track users. Most tools work entirely within your browser.

🔒 In short: we don’t want your data, and we don’t collect it.

Information We Collect

SpeedTools does not collect personal information such as:

  • Names or usernames
  • Email addresses (unless you email us directly)
  • Passwords or login credentials
  • Uploaded files or tool input data

Tool Data & File Processing

All tools on SpeedTools run locally in your browser. Any text, images, PDFs, or other files you use stay on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

Cookies & Tracking

SpeedTools does not use cookies, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or third-party analytics.

Third-Party Services

SpeedTools does not integrate with advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics platforms. External links (such as Instagram) follow their own privacy policies.

Children’s Privacy

SpeedTools does not knowingly collect any personal data from children. Since no accounts or data storage exist, there is no risk of data retention.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, updates will be posted on this page. The core principle of privacy-first and local-only tools will remain.
