About SpeedTools

SpeedTools is a collection of free, fast, and privacy-first online tools — built to help people get things done without logins, ads, or tracking.

Why SpeedTools Exists

Hi, I’m Aryan — a college student with a deep interest in computers and the web.

While working online, I noticed something frustrating: even simple tasks often require creating accounts, accepting tracking, or jumping between multiple websites.

That didn’t feel right. So I decided to build SpeedTools — a place where anyone can use useful tools freely, instantly, and without sacrificing privacy.

What Makes SpeedTools Different

🚀 InstantTools load fast and work instantly.
🔒 PrivateAll processing happens in your browser.
🚫 No loginNo accounts, no tracking, no ads.
📱 UniversalWorks on mobile, tablet & desktop.

What You’ll Find Here

SpeedTools includes text tools, image & PDF utilities, video and audio tools, calculators, converters, and developer helpers — all designed with a clean, premium user experience in mind.

Support SpeedTools

SpeedTools will always remain free. You don’t need to pay to use anything — and you never will.

If you choose to support the project, it helps with hosting, development time, and building better tools in the future.

Support is optional. The site works exactly the same whether you support it or not.

Inspired by the philosophy of tools like VLC and WinRAR — free to use, supported by those who choose to.

Looking Ahead

More tools, better performance, and smoother experiences are planned — while keeping the core promise unchanged: fast tools, zero friction, total privacy.

Built independently with passion, curiosity, and a lot of late nights ☕💻
